book
/bʊk/
noun
A book has pages with words and pictures. You read a book at school.
🔍 Let's look inside the word
b
Letter B Name: "bee" Sound: "buh"
✅
The letter b almost always makes the "buh" sound — like in bat, bed, big, book, bus. Its name is "bee" but its sound is "buh."
oo
Letters OO Name: "oh-oh" Sound: "uh" (short)
✅
Tricky rule! "oo" has two sounds: long "oo" like in moon, food, cool — and short "uh" like in book, look, cook, good. In "book," it's the short "uh" sound (like putting your foot down).
k
Letter K Name: "kay" Sound: "kuh"
✅
The letter k makes the "kuh" sound. In the word "book," the letter c also makes this sound (like in cat, cup, car). Its name is "kay" but its sound is "kuh."
