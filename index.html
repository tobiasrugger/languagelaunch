How it works

01
Student opens the practice site
They pick a unit, a level (A for newcomers, C for intermediate), and an optional translation language.
02
They work through activities
Flashcards, Match, Fill In, True/False, Sort It. In Listen & Repeat, they can record themselves and save.
03
Teacher runs assessment
Students open the Assessment page, type their name, and read words and sentences aloud — no audio model.
04
Teacher reviews in dashboard
All recordings appear in the Teacher Dashboard, organized by student, with inline audio playback.
